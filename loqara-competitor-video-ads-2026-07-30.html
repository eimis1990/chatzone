What Loqara should test next

The most reusable ideas from this swipe file, translated into Loqara-specific creative.

1. Shopify symptom hook

“People visit your store, ask questions, then disappear.” Show Loqara answering a product question and guiding the shopper to the right item.

2. One conversation story

Start with a realistic question, then show product discovery, order lookup, lead capture, or human handoff in under 20 seconds.

3. Bot vs. revenue agent

Contrast a generic FAQ bot with Loqara taking the next commercial action. Make the difference visible, not theoretical.
