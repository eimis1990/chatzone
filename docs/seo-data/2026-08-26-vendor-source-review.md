# Vendor feature and pricing source review — 2026-08-26

This is the dated third-party review required by Phase 0 Task 0.5 of the
[90-day search-visibility plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-08-25-search-visibility-growth.md).
It covers time-sensitive claims used by Loqara's priority vendor, platform, and
alternative comparisons. Public vendor pages remain the authority; quoted or
contract pricing must still be confirmed by the buyer.

## Review method

- Reviewed **26 August 2026**.
- Used current first-party pricing pages, product documentation, and vendor help
  centers. Secondary price estimates were not accepted as authoritative.
- Checked the price unit as well as the headline price: seat, workspace, ticket,
  AI resolution/outcome, conversation, credit, or call minute.
- Corrected public copy where an official source contradicted the article.
- Kept `approximate`/`check current terms` labels because location, billing
  cadence, negotiated contracts, taxes, and packaging can change the final bill.
