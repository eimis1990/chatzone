## 1. What is Loqara?

**Loqara is an AI chat & voice agent for modern e-commerce stores.** It answers customer
questions from *your own* knowledge (not the open internet), searches your product catalog,
looks up order status, captures leads, and hands off to a human when needed — embedded on
your site in **one line of code**.

- **Tagline:** The AI chat & voice agent for modern stores.
- **Website:** https://www.loqara.com
- **Best for:** online stores and brands on Shopify, WooCommerce, or Magento (and any
  website that wants a smart, grounded support agent).
- **Core promise:** answer every customer, day or night — grounded, on-brand, and in your
  customer's language — without making things up.

### Who it's for
Solo founders and small-to-mid e-commerce teams who want to:
- Deflect repetitive support questions (order status, returns, sizing, shipping).
- Capture leads from visitors who'd otherwise bounce.
- Offer instant, 24/7 answers without hiring a night shift.
- Keep a human in the loop for the cases that need one.

## 2. What Loqara does (features)

**Grounded AI chat.** Upload docs, FAQs, or your site and the agent answers from that knowledge
with sources. AI can still be inaccurate; review answers in the inbox and improve the knowledge.
When uncertain, the assistant should say so. Lead capture and live handoff are included from Basic.

**Voice agent (add-on).** A real-time voice agent lets customers *talk* to your store: it
answers spoken questions, searches products out loud, and speaks the customer's language —
right inside the widget. (See Voice add-on pricing below.)

**Commerce skills.** Connect your store and the agent can:
- **Search your live catalog** and show product cards.
- **Look up order status** on Starter and above with WooCommerce or Magento, verified by order number and billing email. Shopify, Verskis and feeds do not support order lookup.
- **Share discount codes** on request.

**Live human handoff (Basic and above).** When a customer needs a person, the bot steps aside and your team
takes over from a real-time inbox — then hands back to the bot when done. Includes
auto-escalation when the bot is stuck.

**Analytics & evaluations (Basic and above).** Every conversation is summarized, tagged with topics, scored for
quality (an AI success rating, 1–5, with reasons), and rolled up into trends — plus CSAT and
fallback rate — so you can improve fast.

**Multilingual & embeddable.** English and Lithuanian out of the box (more on the way). Match
your brand, pick your launcher (circle or pill), and paste a single script tag. The widget
themes itself and speaks your customers' language.

## 3. How it works / setup

1. **Sign up** (free, no credit card) and create your bot.
2. **Add your knowledge** — upload docs/FAQs, point it at your site, or paste Q&A.
3. **Connect your store** (optional) for product search and order lookup.
4. **Customize** the look — colors, avatar, launcher, greeting.
5. **Paste one line of code** before `</body>` on your site. You're live.

- **Self-service setup is free.** Optional done-for-you setup: Essential €749 or E-commerce €995, one-time, in addition to any paid subscription. Setup packages do not override plan or store-provider limits.
- **No developer required** for the basics — copy/paste the snippet. (A developer can paste
  it in seconds if you'd rather.)
- The widget works on any website (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, custom, etc.).

### What counts as a "conversation"?
A text AI session counts once when it produces an automated answer. A new session starts after 24 hours of inactivity or on the first eligible interaction in a new UTC calendar month. Web chat and Messenger use the same rule. Human-only exchanges, previews and empty chat opens do not consume units. The chat history stays available across sessions. **Voice calls are billed separately as minutes** (see add-on).

### What happens if I hit my conversation limit?
At the allowance limit, new AI sessions stop. Existing active sessions and eligible human support can continue. Basic can buy 200 extra conversations for €15; Starter and above can buy 1,000 for €15. Free cannot buy packs. Purchases are manual, with no automatic recharge. Credits expire at the end of the UTC month payment succeeds; the purchased quantity and expiry stay unchanged after a plan change.

---

Existing Free customers receive at least 30 days of in-app notice before the new restrictions take effect. Their actual transition date and temporary allowance are shown in the app. New Free accounts use the new policy immediately. Monthly allowances reset on the first day of each UTC month, including annual subscriptions.

## 9. Data, privacy & security

- **EU-hosted.** Core data (database, auth, file storage) is hosted in the **EU region**.
- **You own your data.** The bot answers **only** from its owner's configured knowledge, and
  your data is **not used to train third-party models**. We **do not sell personal data**.
- **Isolation & encryption.** Data is **encrypted in transit**; each organization's data is
  isolated at the database level with **row-level security**. Embeddable widgets can be
  restricted to an **allowlist of domains**. Server secrets never reach the browser.
- **Retention you control.** Owners set a retention window; conversations older than that are
  **automatically and permanently deleted** by a daily job. You can **export or erase** your
  data on demand at any time. (Custom retention is available on Scale and Enterprise.)
- **What we store:** chat messages, any leads submitted, and aggregate analytics (feedback
  ratings, topic summaries) so the owner can review and improve the bot.
- **Subprocessors:**
  - **Supabase** — database, authentication, and file storage (EU region).
  - **OpenAI** — language-model responses, embeddings, and conversation analysis.
  - **ElevenLabs** — real-time voice (speech-to-text, voice LLM, text-to-speech).
  - **Vercel** — application hosting and content delivery.
- Full details: https://www.loqara.com/privacy and https://www.loqara.com/terms.

## 12. More questions customers ask

**Do I need a developer to install it?**
No. Copy the one-line snippet into your site before `</body>`. On Shopify/WooCommerce/Magento
that's a theme/footer setting; on a custom site it's one line in the HTML.

**Will the bot make things up?**
It is designed to answer from the knowledge you provide and cite sources, but AI can still make mistakes.
Review answers and keep your knowledge current. When unsure, it should say so; on Basic and above
it can also hand off to a human or offer lead capture.

**Can it really see my products and orders?**
Product search is included from Basic on Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, Verskis and supported feeds.
Order lookup requires Starter or above AND WooCommerce or Magento. The order number must match
the billing email. Order lookup is unavailable on Shopify, Verskis and feeds, regardless of tier.

**Can a human take over a chat?**
Yes, on Basic and above. Your team gets a real-time inbox, can take over any conversation, resolve it, and hand it
back to the bot. The bot can also auto-escalate when it's stuck.

**Is my data safe / where is it stored?**
Data is hosted in the EU, encrypted in transit, isolated per organization with row-level
security, and never used to train third-party models. You control retention and can export or
erase data anytime. See the Data & privacy section above.

**Can I try it for free?**
Yes — Free needs no credit card: 50 conversations/month, one bot, one chosen language and basic activity statistics. Live handoff starts with Basic.

**Can I remove the "Powered by Loqara" badge?**
Yes, on Starter and above. Free and Basic keep the Loqara badge.

**How many bots/websites can I run?**
Free and Basic: 1 bot. Starter: 2. Growth: up to 5. Scale & Enterprise: unlimited.

**What if I need more than the plans offer (volume, SSO, SLA)?**
That's Enterprise — custom volume and terms, SSO & SLA, dedicated support. Email
hello@loqara.com.

**Does it work on mobile?**
Yes — the widget is responsive and works on mobile and desktop.

**How do I get help?**
Email **hello@loqara.com**. Growth and above include priority support; Scale and
Enterprise include an SLA.
